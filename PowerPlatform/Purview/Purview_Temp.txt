Sharepoint
================================================

New-complianceSearch `
-Name "Ticket_example" `
-SharepointLocation "https://domain-my.sharepoint.com/personal/Example_domain_com_br" `
-ContentMatchQuery '(documentlink:"https://domain-my.sharepoint.com/personal/Example_domain_com_br/Documents/Documentos/Path/Example/*")'

Start-complianceSearch "Ticket_example"

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Get-complianceSearch "Ticket_example"
| FL Status, Items, ContentMatchQuery, JobStartTime, JobEndTime, ExchangeLocation

New-ComplianceSearchAction `
-SearchName "Ticket_example" `
-Purge `
-PurgeType SoftDelete `
-Confirm:$false

Get-complianceSearchAction "Ticket_example_Purge"
| FL Status, Items, JobStartTime, JobEndTime, ExchangeLocation
